How a value is built

log10(market cap) = 2.9082 + 0.5387 · log10(TVL), fitted on 86 traded comps. Comp market caps are winsorized at their 90th percentile ($104M) when fitting only. One factor, momentum: 1 + 0.5 × (7-day TVL change), clamped to 0.6–1.6. At $100M of TVL the curve pays ×0.165 of TVL.

Nothing else is in the model. Category effects, seasoning, breadth and the sibling factor were each tested out of sample and dropped — see the ablation below. There is no sibling factor in this build at all, so the round-2 self-match bug (a protocol matched against its own name during validation, overstating accuracy ~20%) cannot recur.

The comps

Traded protocols with a market cap, mcap/TVL between 0.01 and 30, and no excluded category (Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC, CEX). That rule keeps 86 comps and excludes 42 traded names. The board is the other 342 protocols that record no token, minus the 144 set aside by rule: 40 watchlist + 158 early = 198 rows.

The clock

Logistic has-token ~ log(age) + category over all 758 protocols. Out-of-sample log-loss 0.624 against a constant 0.688 — a real signal, but a weak one, so it is an informational 12-month launch-odds column only. It never ranks a row and never sets a verdict on its own; it multiplies the expected slice and orders Farm Next.

The farm economics

A $1,000 ticket. Slice = 10% × implied × ($1,000 / TVL). Net = slice × 12-month odds − gas − $5 bridge fees − the 4% T-bill yield forgone over 30 days. Gas per transaction: Ethereum $15, Base $3, Arbitrum $3, Avalanche $2, Polygon $2, Solana $1, Sui $1, other chains $5; two transactions per plan. Hands-on time is 1.5 hours plus 30 minutes per extra chain (capped at 3 extra). ROBUST means net > 0 even at the band low; FARM means net > 0 at the point estimate; THIN means it beats the cash fees only; SKIP means it does not.

Break-even deposit: the deposit D that makes expected net zero at the row’s own implied value, D = costs × TVL / (0.10 × implied × odds). The top rows need deposits of tens of thousands of dollars at a $1,000 ticket — that is what the economics say, and the cards lead with it instead of hiding behind a bare SKIP.

Out of sample — honest numbers

Validation uses the same prediction function as the board: same fit, same momentum, nothing extra. Leave-one-out median miss ×2.98 (51% within 3×, 85% within 10×). Time-split — fit on tokens listed before 2024, test on the newer ones — median miss ×2.00 (67% within 3×). A flat multiple for every protocol misses by ×3.80 leave-one-out and ×3.64 on the time-split: the curve earns its keep, but only just.

The band, stated plainly

Each row carries a P10–P90 band from 200 bootstrap refits of the curve. The band is wide — across the comps the P10–P90 residual factors run 0.18× to 8.28× — and a band built from its own residuals covers ~80% by construction (80.2%). The honest cross-fitted coverage, where each fold’s band limits come from the other folds, is 77.9%. Ranks 1–12 are exact under the 200 refits; the order at the top is not a coin flip.

Where the value sits

Implied value of the 198 pre-token rows, log buckets. Most of the $2.33B sits in a handful of large risk managers; the long tail is where the farm plans that pay live.

What this is not

Implied value is a model, not a price. It says what the market pays for traded protocols of this size, applied to one that records no token. A launch is not promised, the odds column is a weak signal, and a SKIP at $1,000 is the economics talking, not a judgement on the protocol.
